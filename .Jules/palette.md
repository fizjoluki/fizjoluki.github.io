## 2026-06-15 - [Improving Mobile Tap Targets]
**Learning:** For users on mobile devices, especially older patients or those with motor impairments (common in physiotherapy rehabilitation), a small navigation bar can be frustrating and difficult to use. Increasing the hit area to 44px (2.75rem) significantly improves accessibility and usability.
**Action:** Always check the minimum height of interactive elements in mobile views and aim for at least 44px for primary navigation.
